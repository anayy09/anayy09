Most of my work sits in that gap: calibration, deferral, and the unglamorous infrastructure that makes either one reproducible. M.S. Computer Science at [UF](https://www.cise.ufl.edu/), research at [PRISMAp Lab / IC3](https://ic3.center.ufl.edu/) in the College of Medicine.
